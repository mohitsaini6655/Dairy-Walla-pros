Privacy Policy for Dairy Walla

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Dairy Walla" mobile application (the "App") and related services. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Dairy Walla app, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: We may ask you to provide personal details such as your Name, Phone Number, Email Address, and Delivery Address to process your orders and provide our services.
  • Location Data: The App requests access to your location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) to accurately determine your delivery address and track delivery routes. This helps us ensure timely and correct deliveries.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic information about the device you use to access the App, such as device model, operating system, and unique device identifiers to improve app performance.
  • Notifications: We use push notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) to send you updates about your orders, subscriptions, and promotional offers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes, including:

  • To provide and maintain our Service (e.g., milk and dairy product deliveries).
  • To notify you about changes to our Service or updates regarding your orders.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To improve the App's user experience and functionality.

3. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers (like payment gateways or delivery partners) only to the extent necessary to provide our services.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to certain legal obligations).
  • Disable Location tracking and Notifications at any time through your device's system settings, though this may affect the App's functionality.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
