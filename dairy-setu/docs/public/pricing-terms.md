# Pricing & Offer Terms

DairyWalla operates on a freemium model. Our goal is to bring digital transformation to the ground-level distribution network without burdening small businesses.

## For Shopkeepers
**Cost:** 100% Free, Forever.
Shopkeepers will never be charged a subscription fee or commission per order for using the DairyWalla app to place orders with their existing distributors.

## For Distributors

### 1. Basic Plan (Free)
- Up to 50 Shopkeeper Connections
- Basic Order Reception Dashboard
- Standard Push Notifications
- Single Admin Access

### 2. Pro Plan (₹999 / Month)
- Unlimited Shopkeeper Connections
- Advanced Analytics & Sales Tracking
- Custom Product Catalog Upload
- Priority WhatsApp Support
- Downloadable Order Sheets (Excel/CSV)

## Billing Terms
- Subscriptions are billed on a prepaid basis (Monthly or Annually).
- Annual plans include a 20% discount.
- No refunds are provided for mid-month cancellations.

*Prices are exclusive of applicable taxes. Pricing structures are subject to change with 30 days prior notice to existing Pro users.*
