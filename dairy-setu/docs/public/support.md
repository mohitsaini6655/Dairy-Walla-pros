## Technical Support
If you are facing issues with the app, please try the following before contacting support:
1. Ensure your app is updated to the latest version on the Google Play Store.
2. Check your internet connection.
3. Try logging out and logging back in.

If the issue persists, please take a screenshot and send it to our WhatsApp support line along with your registered phone number.
