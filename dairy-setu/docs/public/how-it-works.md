# How It Works: The DairyWalla Flow

DairyWalla digitizes the daily order collection process for milk and ice cream distribution. Here is the exact step-by-step flow of how the platform works:

## Step 1: Distributor Setup
1. The Distributor registers on the app.
2. They set their **Order Cut-off Time** (e.g., 8:00 PM every evening).
3. The app generates a **Unique 8-digit Connection Code** (e.g., X8K2-9M4P).

## Step 2: Shopkeeper Onboarding
1. The Distributor shares the app link and their Connection Code via WhatsApp to all their retailers.
2. The Shopkeeper downloads the app, creates a profile, and enters the Connection Code.
3. The Distributor receives a "Connection Request" on their dashboard and approves it.

## Step 3: Daily Ordering
1. Every evening, the Shopkeeper opens the app and enters the quantity for products (e.g., 20 Amul Taaza, 10 Curd).
2. The Shopkeeper clicks **"Place Order"**.
3. All orders placed before the Cut-off time are aggregated instantly on the Distributor's dashboard.

## Step 4: Fulfillment
1. The Distributor exports or prints the final consolidated order list.
2. The goods are loaded onto the delivery vehicle according to exact requirements.
3. Distributor marks the orders as "Completed" on the dashboard once dispatched.

**No more missed WhatsApp messages, no more confusing phone calls, and no more duplicate entries!**
