# Objection Handling Sheet

*When speaking to Distributors, they will have natural objections. Use this guide to handle them smoothly.*

---

## Objection 1: "Mere shopkeepers ko tech nahi aata, wo WhatsApp hi use karenge."
**Response:** "Sir, ye sabse common doubt hai. Lekin sochiye, aapke shopkeepers PhonePe aur Paytm daily use karte hain na? DairyWalla ka design WhatsApp se bhi zyada simple banaya gaya hai. Bas '+' aur '-' button dabana hai. Hum aapko ek chota sa video denge jo aap unhe forward kar sakte hain."

## Objection 2: "Mai tumhe apna data kyu du? Tum mere retailers chura loge."
**Response:** "DairyWalla ek B2B software (SaaS) company hai, hum dairy suppliers nahi hain. Humara app 'Closed-Loop' hai. Iska matlab jab shopkeeper aapka code dalta hai, to usko app me koi aur distributor dikhega hi nahi. Aapka data 100% encrypted aur safe hai."

## Objection 3: "WhatsApp toh free hai, mai app ke paise kyu du?"
**Response:** "WhatsApp free hai, par usme hone wali galtiyaan free nahi hain. Voice note theek se na sunne par order galat chala jata hai, saman wapas aata hai, aur petrol waste hota hai. Aur sabse badi baat, aapka roz 2 ghante ka time jo bachega, uski value kya hai? Iske alawa, hamara basic plan bilkul free hai, aap free me try karke dekh lijiye."

## Objection 4: "Shopkeepers naya app download karne se mana kar dete hain."
**Response:** "Aapko strict hona padega. Ek baar aap keh denge ki 'Bhaiya, kal se WhatsApp order nahi liya jayega', toh wo majbooran app download karenge. Jab unhe pata chalega ki app me order dena WhatsApp me type karne se bhi jaldi ho jata hai, toh wo khud app prefer karenge."
