# DairyWalla Demo Script

**Duration:** 10-15 Minutes
**Objective:** Make the distributor realize the pain of WhatsApp ordering and show the relief DairyWalla provides.

---

## 1. The Hook (2 mins)
"Sir, how many retailers do you supply to daily?"
*(Wait for answer, e.g., 50)*
"And how do you collect these 50 orders every night? Let me guess... WhatsApp messages, missed phone calls, and voice notes while you are trying to eat dinner with your family?"
*(Nods)*
"How much time does your staff spend just writing these down in a notebook? 2-3 hours? What if I told you we can reduce that to zero?"

## 2. The Reveal (3 mins)
"This is DairyWalla. We are not a marketplace. We don't steal your customers. We are a tool for YOUR business."
*Open the App on your phone/tablet.*
"Look at this dashboard. Imagine waking up at 5 AM, opening the app, and seeing exactly how many crates of milk or boxes of ice cream you need to load. No calculating."

## 3. The Feature Demo (5 mins)
* **Show the Connection Code:** "You generate this code. You give it to your trusted retailers. They lock into your profile. No one else can see your data."
* **Show the Order Placement (Shopkeeper Side):** "Look how easy it is for a shopkeeper. Tap, tap, tap, submit. Even a person who isn't tech-savvy can do it."
* **Show the Cut-off Time:** "My favorite feature: The Cut-Off Time. If you set it to 9 PM, the app literally blocks the shopkeeper from ordering at 9:01 PM. It forces them to be disciplined."

## 4. The Close (2 mins)
"Sir, how much milk gets spoiled or goes unsold because of order miscalculations? If DairyWalla saves you even 2 crates of milk from spoiling a month, the app pays for itself. Let's create your profile right now. It takes 30 seconds."
