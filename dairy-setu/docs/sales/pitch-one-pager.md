# DairyWalla: Distributor Pitch One-Pager

**Target Audience:** Dairy & Ice Cream Distributors  
**Goal:** Hook them in 60 seconds by addressing their biggest pain points.

---

## 🛑 The Problem: Are WhatsApp Orders Killing Your Business?
As a distributor, your evenings are chaotic. Between 7 PM and 10 PM, your phone doesn't stop ringing.
- **Lost Orders:** Mixed up in family group chats and WhatsApp forwards.
- **Voice Notes:** Unclear audio messages ("Bhaiya kal jaisa bhej dena").
- **Late Orders:** Retailers sending orders at 11:30 PM, messing up your morning loading schedule.
- **Wasted Time:** You spend 3 hours every night manually writing orders on paper or Excel.

## ✅ The Solution: Meet DairyWalla 
DairyWalla is a simple app built exclusively for Dairy & Ice-cream distributors to digitize and automate daily order collection.

**How it works in 3 easy steps:**
1. You get a unique 8-digit **Connection Code**.
2. Your shopkeepers download the 100% free app and enter your code.
3. They tap buttons to place orders. You see all orders instantly sorted on one simple dashboard!

## 🚀 Why Distributors Love DairyWalla
- **Zero WhatsApp Mess:** All business orders stay in one professional app.
- **Strict Cut-Off Times:** Set your Order Window (e.g., 9:00 PM). The app blocks shopkeepers from sending late orders.
- **Closed-Loop System:** Your shopkeepers can ONLY see your products and send orders to YOU. Total privacy.
- **Export to Excel:** One click to print the loading sheet for your drivers.
